ElderValley

A multiplayer social RPG on the Base blockchain. Explore a living pixel-art world, fight bosses together, and own a piece of the valley forever.

Phaser 3 · Pixel Art Base Chain · ERC-721 Live on Base · Genesis Mint

Introduction

What is ElderValley and why does it exist.

ElderValley is a browser-based multiplayer RPG where players share a persistent open world rendered in pixel art. The game runs entirely in the browser with no downloads — just open the URL and you are in the valley alongside other players in real time.

The project combines classic RPG exploration with Web3 ownership. Houses in the valley are real NFTs on the Base blockchain. Owning a house means owning a piece of the world: you can customize the interior, set the decorations, and your property persists across all sessions for every visitor.

✅

ElderValley is live on Base mainnet. Core gameplay, multiplayer, and boss fights are playable now, and Genesis houses can be minted on-chain straight from the in-game shop — connect a wallet, pick a house, and confirm the transaction on Base.

Boss Fights

All three bosses are server-authoritative — the server controls AI and HP.

🌋 Lava Golem — Fire Map

A three-phase colossus. Attacks include meteor showers, ground eruptions, and boulder throws. All players in the zone fight it simultaneously. HP is shared — damage dealt by any player counts.

🌿 Swamp Troll — Swamp

Up to 4 trolls patrol the marshlands. Attacks: Toxic Bubble (AoE), Mud Spike (linear), and Sludge Wave (sweeping). Trolls respawn over time. Ambient sounds and bubble warnings signal incoming attacks.

🐝 Bee Queen — Hive

Client-side boss with server-sync for HP. Features voiced dialogue (ElevenLabs TTS, English). Spawns Hive Soldiers mid-fight. Special attacks: Royal Dive, Frantic Dash, Swarm Fury (radial bullet storm).

City

The expanding urban center east of the village.

The City is a secondary social zone with a paved plaza and two knight guards patrolling the streets. Guards speak periodically and can be talked to. Houses placed in the creative editor appear in both zones independently.

Guard patrol routes are algorithmically constrained to the paved road area — they never wander into grass or house zones.

House Ownership

What it means to own a house in ElderValley.

💰 Token Volume Yield

This is the core value of owning a house. 50% of all trading volume fees from the ElderValley token are distributed to house owners. The distribution is weighted by tier — the rarer the house, the greater the share.

TierFee WeightHousesTotal WeightShare of Pool
Common1×252520.7%
Uncommon2×122419.8%
Rare4×83226.4%
Legendary8×54033.1%
Total—50121100%
📈

Example: If the token generates 100 ETH in trading fees, 50 ETH goes to house owners. A Legendary owner receives 8/121 × 50 ETH = 3.3 ETH. A Common owner receives 1/121 × 50 ETH = 0.41 ETH. The more the token trades, the more every house earns.

This creates direct alignment: house owners are incentivized to grow the token's trading volume. The fee weight values are hardcoded in the smart contract via feeWeightOf(tokenId) — tamper-proof and verifiable on-chain.

In-Game Benefits

  • Exclusive entry — private houses reject wallets that don't own them (server-verified)
  • Visitor capacity based on tier: Common 4 · Uncommon 8 · Rare 16 · Legendary unlimited
  • Persistent interior — your decorations and layout are visible to every visitor
  • Creative customization — floors, structures, and lights inside your house

On-Chain Verification

Every house in the game world corresponds to an ERC-721 token on Base. The server runs an indexer watching HousePurchased and Transfer events, keeping ownership always in sync with the chain. Ownership checks happen server-side on every door interaction — not client-side.

Wallet Authentication

Players sign a challenge with MetaMask or Phantom. The server verifies with ethers.verifyMessage and issues a JWT session token valid for 7 days.

⚠️

Guest mode allows exploring the world without a wallet, but house ownership features, profile persistence, and yield distribution require a connected wallet.

Multiplayer

Real-time player sync via WebSocket.

Every connected player streams their position, facing direction, character, and scene to the server every frame via a lightweight WebSocket protocol. The server broadcasts position updates to all players in the same sceneChannel.

Message Types

TypeDirectionDescription
helloClient → ServerJoin with wallet auth + initial position
stateClient → ServerPosition update every frame
chatClient → ServerBroadcast a chat message
hitBossClient → ServerPlayer dealt damage to a boss
welcomeServer → ClientSession confirmed + current peers
bossSyncServer → ClientBoss HP, phase, position (100ms)
storageUpdatedServer → ClientCreative world save broadcast
roomFullServer → ClientHouse at visitor capacity

Anti-Cheat

All boss HP and damage resolution runs server-side. Client sends hitBoss(damage) — the server clamps damage to a maximum of 500 per hit and applies it to the authoritative HP value. Clients only render what the server broadcasts.

ElderValleyHouses (ERC-721)

  • ERC-721 Enumerable — full ownership enumeration
  • 12 house types defined in constructor (genesis supply hard-capped)
  • ETH forwards instantly to treasury — contract never holds funds
  • Tier system: Common (0), Uncommon (1), Rare (2), Legendary (3)
  • Pausable by owner (emergency only)
  • Emits HousePurchased event indexed by the game server

ElderValleyHouseMarketplace

  • Secondary market for house NFTs
  • 2.5% platform fee (configurable 0–10% by owner)
  • Fee distributed proportionally to house tier weights
  • ReentrancyGuard on all payment flows
